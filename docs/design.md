# Pocket Life: design notes and history

The full record of what's been decided and built, by area. CLAUDE.md has the short version; read the section here for the area you're working on.

Luke's mobile "life in your pocket" game: SEED meets One Hour One Life. Built step by step with Claude.

## Design decisions (keep to these)
- Soft low-poly 3D like SEED, minimal UI like One Hour One Life. Luke is very particular about the look: don't restyle without asking.
- One person per player. When you die you start as a new person in the same world.
- Real-time 24-hour clock and real dates, one clock for the whole world (0.1.6): settings.worldTimeZone, Australia/Darwin for the Oceania world (worldNow() in game.js), so day and night are the same for everyone. Regions (decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026): Oceania, Europe, Americas, each its own world on its own time. The world picker sits under Play on the main menu (0.1.7, regions in game-data.json): only Oceania is open; tapping Europe or Americas shows this world's view at that region's time of day (worldNow(tz), menuRegion) with Play saying '<Region> is coming soon'. A world that isn't open yet is drawn fresh (0.2.1, Luke): every tree standing, bushes full, grass grown, no items, campfires or people (setFresh in screens.js, freshLook in core.js; drawing only). Later each will be its own Durable Object world.
- Age is chosen when making a person (planned: 13 to 90, was 18 to 90) and goes up in real time, a year per real year. Male or female, male is the default with short hair.
- Players are 13+. Deaths: hunger, thirst, tiredness, old age, animals, accidents. No PvP.
- Home storage can't be taken, but if a player is gone too long and upkeep lapses, it goes out to the world.
- Start-from-nothing world. Every item has a weight (stick 1, stone 1). Carry capacity 30.
- Needs are realistic (0.0.7): hunger lasts about a day, thirst about 16 hours awake. Berries are picked one at a time from berry bushes into your inventory and eaten one at a time (10 hunger each). A full bush holds 5, and picked berries grow back one by one (a bare bush is full again after regrowHours, 4). One drink at the pond gives 15 thirst. No water for about 3 days or no food for about a week and you die. About 30 berry bushes (berryBushes.extra in game-data.json adds them in the wider world on their own random numbers).
- Your person walks round rocks, bushes, logs, stumps, tree trunks and the pond: walks find a route on a half-metre grid (A*, findPath in game.js) and cut corners where the line is clear; stuck for 1.5 s means one new route, then "Can't get there". People strolling while away follow the same routes on screen.
- Controls (same on phone and computer): tap the ground for a small menu with Walk here (a faint ring shows where); a quick double-tap walks straight there. Tap or click a stick, stone, berry bush or the pond to open a small menu (Pick up Stick, Harvest Berries, Drink, Walk here); right-click or holding a finger down opens it too. A walk joins the end of the task queue, so it never interrupts anything. A small list above the bottom bar shows what your person is doing now and what's next (hidden when there's nothing to do), with an x on each task and Stop all. UI style (0.0.9, Luke's pick): dark glass like SEED, as little on screen as possible, icons only. Info card top left (name · age, date and time, online dot), Menu round button top right, needs as rings with icons bottom left (tap for names and numbers; a low need pulses red, under 10% it flashes), round Say, Craft (hammer) and Inventory (bag with a weight badge) buttons bottom right. Say opens a chat bar along the bottom (kept above the keyboard with visualViewport) with the last 6 lines above it; it stays open after sending and closes when you tap the world or press Escape; the bottom buttons hide while chatting. All pop-ups, sheets, tap menus, labels and the Make your person sheet use the same glass (style.css has the theme at the top). Don't restyle without asking. Main menu (0.1.3): the first screen, with Play (shows 'as Name · age' or 'Make your person'), What's new, Settings (Graphics) and Feedback over a live view of the world, the camera slowly circling the main pond (menuCamera). The menu joins the world as a watcher (hello with watch: true; no person, not counted as playing, its messages ignored except feedback); Play sends a second hello on the same connection. Escape, or the Menu button, opens the pause menu: Resume, Exit (back to the main menu, your person goes away; server leave()) and Admin for the admin. Make your person has a Back button. There's no New person: you get a new person when yours dies or leaves the world.  On a computer, W A S D move the camera, Q and E turn it round your person, and R and F tilt it.
- While testing, settings.autoName fills in a name from testNames on the Make your person screen.
- Online (started in 0.0.6): one world for everyone. Both links join the same world (the live one): the test link is only for trying a new build in the real world before everyone gets it. Keep test builds' world layout the same as the release, or shared items and bushes won't line up. Other people show with a name tag and walk about; the Say button (or Enter) shows a speech bubble over your head. Sticks, stones and berries are shared: the server holds them, picking up or harvesting asks the server (if someone beat you to it, it comes back out of your inventory), and drops go through the server. Online, the world tops up one stick and one stone every respawnMinutes (10) while there are fewer on the ground than startInWorld: sticks under trees, stones by boulders (later this slows down once sticks come from trees and stones from rocks). The first player into a new world sends its starting layout (seed). Your person, needs and inventory live on the server (0.0.7), tied to the phone's random id (no sign-in yet). The HUD shows a dot (green online, orange connecting, grey offline) and how many are online. The Menu has Graphics (Low, Medium, High, saved on the device) and Send feedback (stored on the server; read it in Admin, Feedback, reports, players). Chat safety: bad words in speech bubbles show as stars, and a bad name gets 'Pick a different name' (chatFilter in game-data.json; tools/worlds.py test-build copies it to server/words.json for the server). Tap another person for Mute (hides their bubbles, saved on the phone), Report (reason plus note, stored on the server with what they said lately) and Walk here. Names also can't contain admin, mod and similar (chatFilter.names). The server limits how fast each player can chat, pick up, harvest, drop, send feedback and report (LIMITS in server/src/shared.js); too fast gets 'Slow down a little'. Each phone has a random id; others only see a scrambled tag of it. Decorative pebbles, flint and the coloured flowers are no longer drawn (still laid out, so nothing else moves) because they looked like stones you could pick up. Admin player: typing /admin and the code in the Say box (never said out loud) makes that phone the admin, remembered on the phone (/admin off forgets it). The code is the Cloudflare secret ADMIN_CODE that Luke set; it's never in the repo. The Menu then has Admin: maintenance on or off with a message (release-link players see Back soon; the test link and the admin still get in), message everyone (a banner), Feedback, reports, players (tabs: Feedback, Reports, Online, Banned), admin tag on or off (shows 'Name · Admin'), and Reset the world (tap twice; everyone rejoins and the first lays out the starting layout). Tapping the ground, a person or an item as admin adds an Admin item (Add <item>, Teleport here; Mute for everyone, Kick, Ban, Teleport to them; Remove). Next: trading (both players accept). No direct giving for now.
- Away from the game (built in 0.0.7, run by the server): your person stays in the world 24/7. For the first 24 hours away they keep themselves going: stroll within away.wanderMax (25 m) of the main pond (away.near), eat from their inventory, pick berries from nearby bushes, and walk to the pond to drink. If they still run out of food or water they can die (water is always there; add more berry bushes if too many die). After 24 hours away they lie down asleep on the ground (needs pause). After another day asleep (away.goneAfterDays 1, so 48 hours away in all, Luke's pick 0.1.4) they're gone and their inventory drops where they slept; Welcome back says how long you have. Coming back shows a Welcome back card. Others see 'away' or 'away · fast asleep' under the name; sleepers lie down. A grave marks where someone died for a day. Admin extras: tap a person for Fill their needs, Wake up, Send to sleep, Remove from world; tap yourself for Fill my needs; Speed up time (60x for 10 min) in the Admin panel; delete feedback and reports. The Menu's What's new shows devlog from game-data.json (add an entry for every version). No offline play: without the server the game shows Joining or Reconnecting. When the game is hidden (another app, phone locked) it says bye and closes, so the person goes away at once; it pings every 25 s and the server closes connections quiet for 90 s. The server ticks every 20 s. Others' actions (bend, pick, kneel to drink) are shown via 'act' messages; away people's strolls carry 'then' so watchers see them drink or pick on arrival. Admin options in tap menus sit behind one 'Admin' item (a submenu with Back). Autopilot (0.0.8 patch, in the game, not the server): in the game but no taps for away.idleMinutes (3) and nothing queued, your person eats, drinks, picks berries and strolls near where you left off, using the normal task queue; an 'On autopilot' chip shows and any tap takes over. Strollers seen on screen follow routes round rocks and the pond (findPath): with a route they keep strictly to it (no sidestepping), without one they retry every second, they never start inside a solid (outsideSolid), and stroll messages carry ms (time to arrive) so their game speeds them up (up to 2.5x) to keep in step with the server (0.1.7). The strolling check in tools/tests watches this.

Below slowBelow (20) you're 'Out of breath' and walking and tasks take slowBy (1.5) times as long (slowness() in game.js; walks send sp so others see the slower walk). Never stops you, not deadly. Health heals 4 an hour (8 asleep) when nothing is empty.

Tools wear through wearTool() on the server.   - While away and asleep (after the first 24 h), hunger and thirst keep dropping slowly (at the asleep rates) but stop at a 10% floor, so away people can't die; they wake hungry and thirsty.
  - Sleep gets a bonus at night (Darwin clock): sleep any time, but Rest fills faster at night. Away and autopilot people go to sleep when it gets dark.
  - Clock stays real Darwin time. Ageing is real time: age goes up 1 each real year on the person's birthday (the day they were made). Old age death left out for now.
  - How it's built: away.goneAfterDays 2 (after sleepAfterHours 24) and away.asleepFloor 10 (advanceStats floor: needs with atZero stop there or where they were, no harm, no death; caught up lazily when they come back). night {from 20, to 6} in game-data.json plus settings.worldTimeZone (server: isNight, morningAfter, hourAt in needs.js); rest.whileAsleepAtNight 16 (full in about 6 h). Away and autopilot people sleep when night starts until morning. ageOf(p) in needs.js: p.age plus birthdays since p.born (Darwin date); sent in me, pub look, yours and died; the game shows Happy birthday.

## How the code is laid out
- Files (the same set in test/, the root and releases/<version>/): index.html is the page and a small loader; style.css is the look of the menus and buttons; game/*.js is the game code, one file per topic (three.js r170 from jsDelivr through an import map); game-data.json is all the content. No build step.
- The loader fetches game-data.json fresh, then style.css and the game's parts with its version and build added to their addresses, so phones never mix old and new files. The parts (listed in PARTS in index.html, in order) share one scope: the loader joins them and runs them as one module, exactly like one file. So a part can use anything from an earlier part; keep things in the part for their topic, and add a new part to PARTS (and release.sh copies the whole game/ folder). The parts: core (helpers, renderer, data), land, nature (trees, rocks, bushes, pond plants), plants (tall grass and other cuttable plants), world-build, items (shapes, items, recipes, needs), people (bodies), world-items (things on the ground), you, needs (needs ticking, day and night), inventory (and craft), camera (and graphics), moving (walking, pathfinding, the task queue, tasks, autopilot), input (taps, tap menus, pause, keys), screens (make your person, death, main menu), loop, online (networking and the server's messages), admin, others (other people, walking about, chat), start.
- How to add things: an item or recipe is a game-data.json edit (items, recipes; see its help). A cuttable plant like tall grass is a plants entry. Then run python3 tools/worlds.py test-build so the server knows. A new kind of action needs a handler in server/src/actions.js, a task in game/moving.js (the queue, nextTask) and a tap menu entry in game/input.js (menuActions). Placeables and buildings will get their own shared system (things placed in the world with a kind, position and state, in data) when the first one is built; build it generic so the next ones are data entries.
- game-data.json: all content. Items, recipes, needs (stats), things to use (sources), day and night colours, character looks, body shapes, settings. It has a "help" section explaining each part.
- Adding content should almost always be a small edit to game-data.json, not code. New items automatically appear in the admin's Add list, the Inventory and Craft (with a picture drawn from the 3D model); keep it that way for placeables and buildings when they come.
- The world is settings.worldSize metres square (100 for now), with thick forest past the edge. The clearing round the start is laid out from its own random numbers and the rest of the world from a second set, so changing the world never reshuffles the clearing.
- Keep it light for phones. Lots of copies of a shape are drawn as one InstancedMesh, split into chunks of the map. Grass is only drawn near the camera. Far-off trees use simpler shapes.
- Since 0.0.7 nothing about the game is saved on the phone except settings (graphics, mutes, the admin code) and the phone's random id. The server holds people (one storage key per person, p:<id>), the world, feedback and reports.
- server/ is the online world: a Cloudflare Worker (named pocket-life) with one Durable Object holding the whole world (named live). src/index.js is the World (storage, sockets, hello, the message pipeline, the tick, leaving); its other methods are in files by topic added onto it (persons.js, away.js, nature.js, line.js, admin.js); src/actions.js has one small handler per player message ({ when: any|alive|world, run }); src/shared.js has what they all use (game.json rules, LIMITS, helpers); src/needs.js is needs and inventory maths; src/filter.js the chat filter; game.json and words.json are copied from test/game-data.json by tools/worlds.py test-build (so run it after changing needs, items, sources, away or chatFilter). PROTOCOL in index.js and proto in the game's hello must match. It runs at wss://pocket-life.misty-glade-6e8e.workers.dev/world. Cloudflare builds and deploys it from the server folder on every push to main. The game finds it through "server" in game-data.json.
- Cloudflare free plan (Luke is on it, 0.1.4): 100,000 requests a day (20 incoming WebSocket messages count as 1, plus connections and alarms) and 100,000 storage writes a day; past either, things fail until 00:00 UTC (9:30 am Darwin).
