# Societies: your claim is your society

- Players see a society as a camp (Luke's pick, 24 Nov 2026, built 0.1.6): Menu > Camp, Invite to camp, "Your camp", End camp. The code, the server's internals and the admin still say society and claim (wording.md).
- First built in 0.1.4 (24 Nov 2026), reworked in 0.1.5 the same day (Luke: "lighting starts society", "claim/society same same"). Before that (23 Nov) he picked societies as player groups inside the main world, not player-run worlds. Rules in game-data.json "societies" (maxMembers, nameMax, inviteMinutes, inviteReach). The server decides everything (server/src/societies.js, handlers in actions.js, claims.js tryClaim and claimLost); the game shows it (game/society.js).
- Your claim is your society. Lighting your first campfire starts both: the society is called "<your person's name>'s camp" (a number on the end if that's taken) until the leader renames it, and th.claim is 's:<id>'. The fire going out ends both, whatever puts it out (burnt out, the leader, the admin). There's no Start step any more (0.1.4 had one in the Menu).
- One claim each (Luke): your own society's, or the one you're in. Any other fire you light is just a fire.
- Only the leader can put the society's fire out, since that ends it (Claude's call with the rework, so a member can't end it for everyone). Any member can feed it, sit by it and cook on it.
- Size: 3 people for now (Luke: "Maybe 3 Max for now"; a 10 m claim only fits a fire, 2 or 3 shelters and a small farm). Also a live setting: Admin > Settings, Players, "People in a society" (societyMax). Lowering it never takes anyone out; it only stops new invites.
- Joining (Luke's pick): by invite. The leader taps a person nearby (within inviteReach, 8 m) who is playing, Invite to camp; they get a card at the top, Join or No thanks, for inviteMinutes (5). The invite waits on their connection, so it needs no storage write. Someone alone in their own society puts their fire out first (that ends it: Luke, "lighting the fire is what activates the claim, putting out the fire deactivates"); someone in one with others leaves it first. The card says which, and the same invite still works afterwards.
- Leader (Luke's pick): the leader invites, takes people out (Remove, two taps) and renames it. Anyone can leave (two taps). If the leader leaves or is gone, the member who joined first leads and the fire burns on. When the last one leaves (the button says End it), the society ends and its fire goes out (Luke's pick). When the admin ends one, its fire goes out too.
- Membership is by player, so it stays when your person dies and you start again. A player with no person in the world for 3 days (the away timer: away too long, or dead and not back) leaves their society (socGone in the server's tick).
- Can it live for ever? (Luke asked.) No: away members only feed the fire from what's already in their bags, they fall asleep after a day away and stop, a fire holds 3 days at most, and members gone for 3 days leave. It lasts only while someone keeps playing and bringing fuel. The tick checks every claim's fire, so one that burns out ends its society within a minute.
- Names: 2 to 20 letters (a default name can be a bit longer), through the chat filter, no two the same (any case). Shown under each member's name tag (Luke's pick) once there are 2 or more in it, with away or asleep after it. A claim is called by its society's name: walking into one shows "Your camp" or its name, and its tap menu says "<name> · Only its members can use things here".
- Where: Menu > Camp (the pause menu's tiles are two rows of two: Camp, What's new, Settings, Feedback). Not in one: how to start one (light a campfire). In one: its name (with a Rename box for the leader), who leads it, the members (leader first) with Remove for the leader, and Leave or End it.
- Admin: World tab, Societies: each one with its leader, how many of how many, and its claim; Fly to, Members (each with Take out, and a Rename box), End (two taps: its fire goes out). End claim on a fire ends its society too (the fire burns on). Give claim makes a new society for that person (not someone already in one). A player's page shows their society with Take out of society. The Log has started, joined, left, taken out, renamed, ended and the admin's tools.
- Claims from before 0.1.5 (a player's id) became their owner's society when the server started; a 0.1.4 society with no burning fire ended (socTidy).
- Checks: tools/tests/societies.mjs (the server, no browser, part of server_tests) and the societies check (the game in Chrome, with the admin's list).
- Not built yet: storage and shelter shared by a society, trading, a claim that grows with the society.
