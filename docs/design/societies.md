# Societies: groups of players sharing one claim

- Built in 0.1.4 (decided with Luke 24 Nov 2026). Before that (23 Nov) he picked societies as player groups inside the main world, not player-run worlds. Rules in game-data.json "societies" (maxMembers, nameMax, inviteMinutes, inviteReach). The server decides everything (server/src/societies.js, handlers in actions.js); the game shows it (game/society.js).
- Size: 3 people for now (Luke: "Maybe 3 Max for now"; a 10 m claim only fits a fire, 2 or 3 shelters and a small farm). Also a live setting: Admin > Settings, Players, "People in a society" (societyMax). Lowering it never takes anyone out; it only stops new invites.
- Land (Luke's pick): one society claim. Only the leader's lit campfire holds land for the whole society (th.claim is 's:<id>' instead of a player's who). Members can do everything inside it, any member can feed and use its fire, and members' own fires are just fires. Starting a society while you have a claim makes your claim the society's.
- Joining (Luke's pick): by invite. The leader taps a person nearby (within inviteReach, 8 m) who is playing, Invite to society; they get a card at the top, Join or No thanks, for inviteMinutes (5). The invite waits on their connection, so it needs no storage write.
- Someone with their own claim (Luke): must put their fire out first, since lighting a fire starts a claim and putting it out ends it. The card says so, and the same invite still works once it's out.
- Leader (Luke's pick): the leader invites and takes people out; anyone can leave (two taps). If the leader leaves or is gone, the member who joined first leads. The last one out ends the society and keeps its claim as their own. When the admin ends one, its claim goes to the leader.
- Membership is by player, so it stays when your person dies and you start again. A player with no person in the world for 3 days (the away timer: away too long, or dead and not back) leaves their society (socGone in the server's tick).
- Can it live for ever? (Luke asked.) No: away members only feed the fire from what's already in their bags, they fall asleep after a day away and stop, a fire holds 3 days at most, and members gone for 3 days leave. It lasts only while someone keeps playing and bringing fuel.
- Names: 2 to 20 letters, through the chat filter, no two the same (any case). Shown under each member's name tag (Luke's pick), with away or asleep after it, and as the claim's name ("Rivers's claim"; members see "Your society's claim").
- Where: Menu > Society (the pause menu's tiles are now two rows of two: Society, What's new, Settings, Feedback). Not in one: a name box and Start a society. In one: its name, who leads it, the members (leader first) with Remove for the leader, and Leave.
- Admin: World tab, Societies: each one with its leader, how many of how many, and its claim; Fly to, Members (each with Take out, and a Rename box), End (two taps). A player's page shows their society with Take out of society. The Log has started, joined, left, taken out, ended and the admin's tools.
- Checks: tools/tests/societies.mjs (the server, no browser, part of server_tests) and the societies check (the game in Chrome, with the admin's list).
- Not built yet: storage and shelter shared by a society, trading, a claim that grows with the society, more than one society claim, sharing fires with friends outside a society.
