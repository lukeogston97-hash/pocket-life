# Inventory, Craft and tap menus

- Inventory, Craft and tap menus (old 0.2.5, Luke approved a mockup; built so new items, recipes and placeables need no UI work):
  - Inventory is a grid of tiles, 5 across, at least 10 slots (empty dashed ones fill out two rows, Luke's pick over 20 to keep it small). Order: food, tools, things you place, then the rest, each in game-data.json order. A tile shows the picture, a count when more than 1 and a wear bar for tools. Each tool gets its own tile (Luke) with its own wear (old 0.2.6): p.wear[kind] is a list of uses left, the one in use first (tools past its end are new; saves from before had one number; wearList in server/src/needs.js and game/inventory.js). Any tool can be dropped (drop items carry which); a dropped tool keeps its wear on the ground (item.uses) and when picked up, and so do tools spilled when someone dies or is gone. It opens with nothing picked; tap a tile for an info bar under the grid (name, weight, uses left, its about line, the main thing in green: Place or Eat, then Drop 1 / Drop all and Craft with ›), tap it again to hide it. Items can have "about" (else the recipe's about is used).
  - The craft mat (0.1.8, Luke's idea, 25 Nov 2026; replaced the Craft sheet of recipe cards and its all-but-one discovery from old 0.2.7): Craft is in the inventory, a Craft button in its header. The hammer button at the bottom right was taken out before the 0.1.8 release (Luke: not needed now Craft is in the inventory). The mat sits above the bag: tap a tile to put one on it, or drag it there (touch: hold first); tap it on the mat to take it back. Nothing leaves the bag until Make (the grid just shows what's left). When what's on the mat is exactly a recipe's needs plus keeps (matIs in rules.js), Make lights up: one the player has never made shows as "Something new" (a ? and a glowing Make) and making it is a discovery ("New! It's in your Found list now", the toast says Discovered). Otherwise the mat says "Something's missing" (all of it is part of a recipe: matPartOf) or "Nothing happens". Failed tries use nothing up. Under the mat: Bag or Found. Found (Luke's pick) lists the recipes you've made, ready ones first, with need chips (green when enough), the Tools / Food / Building / Clothes / Materials buttons once there are 2 kinds and search from settings.craftSearchFrom (15), and how many are left to discover; tap one and its things go on the mat, with what's missing shown faded (+1). Found recipes are p.found on the server, added on each first craft, and carry over to your next person (Luke: whatever was easier; it was a one-liner in create). Station recipes (cooking at a campfire) count as known without being found, so the fire's Cook menu and dragging raw fish onto a fire still work for everyone. Since 0.1.9 they're never made on the mat (food only at stations): the mat shows what it makes and "Cook this on a lit campfire", with Make off, and Found lists mat recipes only. The empty mat adds "Try two stones to start" until the first discovery. Craft with › in the inventory opens the mat with one of that thing on it.
  - Recipes list, no mystery (0.1.10 test 9, Luke, 25 Nov 2026: he liked the mat but had no idea how to make a campfire, and lots more recipes are coming). Under the mat the tabs are Inventory and Recipes (were Bag and Found; Luke: Bag isn't the right word). Recipes lists every mat recipe: the ones you've made first, with their need chips, then the rest greyed out, just a picture and a name (Luke's pick), ready ones first in each; the kind buttons and search count all of them. Tapping any one, made or not, puts its things on the mat, as many as it takes, with what you don't have faded (+n) (Luke: show the qty). The mat always shows the real picture and name of what's on it, never "Something new" or a "?" (Luke: no mystery), and Make no longer glows. The first make says "Your first one!" and the toast "Made your first ...". Kept: p.found is what you've made (it greys a recipe in or out; admin's Find all and Clear found set it), and the empty mat's "Try two stones to start" until your first make. Luke turned down world firsts (the first maker's name on a recipe) and a separate Ideas tab.
  - Greyed out by what you carry, and a tapped recipe stays put (0.1.12 test 4, Luke, 25 Nov 2026). Luke: tapping Campfire said sharp stone, Fishing spear and Stone knife said stone axe head (with only 2 stones, or a sharp stone and twine, the part you have is exactly another recipe). Now the recipe you tap is what the mat shows until you change what's on it, with what's missing faded and Make off; Make only makes that recipe (matMakes in inventory.js). Greyed out now means you don't carry everything it takes (kept tools too), not that you haven't made it (Luke: "not greyed out means you have all them items"): the ones you have everything for come first with their need chips, the greyed ones just a picture and a name, each in game-data.json order. Too heavy doesn't grey a recipe (you have the things; the mat says Too heavy to carry). The line under the list says "Greyed out means you don't have everything for it yet. Tap one to see what it needs", and hides when nothing is greyed. p.found now only gives "Your first one!", the toast and the empty mat's tip.
  - Two tabs, no Craft button (0.1.15, Luke, 25 Nov 2026). Luke: with every recipe listed, the mat on the Inventory tab and the Craft button were useless now, but he still liked the mat on Recipes. The inventory sheet has two tabs at the top in place of its title, Inventory and Recipes (the same pill buttons that sat under the mat), with Close on the right; it always opens on Inventory. Inventory is just the grid and its info bar. Recipes is the craft mat with the list under it. Also gone (Luke): Craft with › in an item's info bar, and the mat's "Something's missing / These go with something else" (and "Nothing happens"), since things can't be put on the mat by hand any more: no tapping or dragging tiles onto it, and its tiles are just pictures (no tap to take one off). The mat shows only the recipe tapped in the list, worked out again on every draw from what you carry (fitMat), so it keeps up when you pick things up or Make; Clear empties it. The empty mat says "Tap a recipe to put its things here" (plus "Try a sharp stone first" until your first make), and the first-steps tip says "tap Recipes". matPartOf left rules.js.
  - The inventory and craft lists only redraw when what they show changes (server updates come often and would swap the buttons out from under a tap).
  - Tap menus: options marked foot (Inspect, Walk here) sit small along the bottom of every menu; past 5 others the rest go under More ›; Admin always shows. Walk here stays the main option where it's the only one (the ground).
  - Campfire menu: the title shows lit and time left, or the fuel in it. Order: Light fire, Add fuel › (each fuel you carry: add 1, or as many as fit), Cook ›, Sit by the fire, Put out fire, then Inspect and Walk here along the bottom.
  - Stations (groundwork for cooking): a recipe with "near": "campfire" can only be made within 3 m of the edge of a built one, lit if it's a fire (stationNear in placeables.js; the server allows 4 m, stationFor in placed.js). Found and the mat show a 'By a lit campfire' chip or 'Cook it by a lit campfire', and the placeable's tap menu lists those recipes under its craftLabel (Cook) as a make task that walks over first.
  - Pack up and Take apart (old 0.2.6, Luke): once a built placeable is out (a fire not lit) and nobody else is sitting by it, the person who built it (th.by, set on place; older ones have none, so anyone) or the admin can Pack up (the item back in the inventory; fuel left is lost) or Take apart (its recipe's parts land on the ground round it; once a fire has been lit (th.burnt), the parts that burn are gone, so a campfire gives back its 2 stones; twigs burn too, 0.1.8). Server: unplace (now also built ones) and apart, whyNotTakeDown in placed.js. A packed-up campfire placed again counts as unused.
  - Dragging from the bag (old 0.3.6, Luke): a tile can be dragged (mouse after 8 px, touch after a 260 ms hold so the grid still scrolls) onto the world; the bag fades while dragging and reopens after. Your person: eat (food only). A built campfire: fuel adds 1, a raw food its recipe starts cooking (needs lit). Anywhere else: drop 1 (a tool drops that one). Off the world: nothing. bagDragOn / bagDropAt in inventory.js.
- Glow (old 0.3.7, Luke 23 Nov 2026): to show what you're about to click, loose items (sticks, stones, tools) get a faint warm outline when the mouse is over them, and the thing a tap menu is open for stays outlined (phone and computer). Code: end of game/input.js (updateGlow). Luke decided (23 Nov 2026): only loose items glow, not campfires (old 0.3.8), trees, rocks, boulders, stumps or bushes.
  - Drops land where you let go (Luke, 23 Nov 2026): a drag onto the ground drops there, walking over first if it's far (drop task with x, z; the server only trusts a spot within reach, else it lands by your feet). A loose item on the ground can be dragged too (input.js wdrag: mouse after 8 px, touch after a 300 ms hold): it queues pick up, then drop there, add fuel or cook. The check does not cover the loose-item drag yet.
  - Drags make tasks (Luke, 23 Nov 2026, 0.1.2 test 2): a drag from the bag onto the ground is a drop task at the end of the list (like Walk here), not a drop straight away; Drop 1 and Drop all in the info bar still drop by your feet at once. Drops with a spot show in the task list ("Drop Stick") and get their number over the spot (numbers show from 2 tasks, like the rest), and a see-through copy of the item (seeThrough in world-items.js, dropGhosts in moving.js) sits where it will land, turned the way it will lie, until it's down. Moving a loose item is pick up, then the next step with from: its id, skipped if someone else took it first. Letting go on the pond or a bush says Can't put it there. While dragging, the view doesn't turn (controls.enableRotate off); near the edge of the screen (12%) it slides that way (edgePan in input.js), only once the drag has been away from the edge. Later: dragging onto storage gets a put-in task (marked in bagDropAt).
  - 0.1.2 test 3 (Luke, 23 Nov 2026): a task's number shows even when it's the only one left (for every task), so a move's number stays till the item is down (a drop's number goes with its see-through copy). A loose item already queued to be picked up (queuedItem in moving.js) can't be dragged, tapped or right-clicked again (it says Already in your task list, and doesn't glow) till its task is done or taken off the list; taking a pick-up off the list takes its move's second half with it. From the bag you can't queue more drops of a kind than you carry (spareInBag).

## The tool bar (0.1.17, Luke, 26 Nov 2026)
- Why: tools took up lots of the inventory's squares and weight, and more tools (and maybe clothes and backpacks) are coming. Luke looked at mockups of clothes slots, a belt and a person button for clothes, then had those taken out: just tools, in their own smaller bar.
- Look (Luke approved a mockup): a row of smaller squares (7 across, gap 6 px) above the inventory grid, the tools first in game-data.json order, then dashed empty spots. The weight moved up into the header between the tabs and Close (Inventory tab only), so the sheet is only about 20 px taller than before. On a phone under 360 px wide the word "Weight" is left out.
- Rules (Luke's picks):
  - 7 spots (settings.toolSlots, also a live setting: Admin > Settings > Players > Tool bar spots). More tools than that and players choose what to carry; the rest go in the inventory or get left at camp.
  - Only tools on the bar get used, still by themselves (tap a tree and the axe comes out): no picking a tool per task. A tool in the inventory squares does nothing until it's equipped: the tap menu says "Equip your stone axe".
  - Tools on the bar weigh nothing (Luke: they shouldn't count). In the inventory they weigh as normal.
  - Tools can be crafted and kept in the inventory (Luke: a toolmaker can carry axes to trade). Equip: tap one in the inventory, the green button is Equip; or drag it onto the bar. Unequip: tap one on the bar; or drag it onto the inventory. A full bar: drag a tool onto one on the bar to swap them.
  - A tool you make or pick up goes straight on the bar while there's room (so a new player who makes an axe can chop with it), else into the inventory.
  - Spares: allowed, each in its own spot. The first of a kind on the bar is the one in use. When it breaks the next one on the bar takes over; with none, a spare of the same tool in the inventory moves up into its spot (how it worked before the bar).
- Code: rules.js (barUsed, bagWeight, tidyBar: the same on both sides). The server keeps p.bar = { kind: n }, part of p.carry: the first p.bar[kind] of a tool's wear list are the bar's (server/src/needs.js barOf, addCarry, takeCarry, equipTool, unequipTool; the equip and unequip messages in actions.js). Someone from before the bar gets their tools put on it the first time the server looks (barOf). An older game still works: it sees the tools in carry as before. The game does the same straight away (you.js addToCarry, takeFromCarry, carried; inventory.js barSlots, equipTool, unequipTool, toolDropAt) and the server's answer has the last word. tools/tests/toolbar.mjs checks the server's side.
- The ground's tap menu (0.1.18): Dig farm field while you carry a stone hoe (greyed out outside your camp, or with the hoe not on the tool bar), then Walk here small along the bottom. With no hoe it's just Walk here, as before (docs/design/farming.md).
