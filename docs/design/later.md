# Planned and not built yet: sign-in, buildings, later ideas, stone

- Sign-in (decided 21 Nov 2026, not built yet): Google sign-in later, optional, so the same person works on phone and PC; signing in keeps the person you already have. The server's whoFor(m) in index.js is where it plugs in (map 'acct:<google id>' to the phone's who). Luke makes the Google Cloud web client ID when we build it.
- Buildings: not built yet; use the placeables system.
- Later: everything saved on the server, day planner, skills, more stats, regions and time zones, app store wrapper.
- Stone (0.2.9, Luke): loose stones are picked up by hand and still top up as a safety net. Rocks need the stone pickaxe: Break rock gives 5 stones and the rock is gone, but one broken rock comes back (same spot, beside its boulder) every mining.rockBackHours (4). Boulders are permanent and can't be removed: Mine boulder gives 3-5 stones, then it looks worn down and can be mined again after regrowDays (3, same as trees). Later: better pickaxes, ore, quarries; maybe slow the loose-stone top-up once mining is the main source.
- Societies (decided with Luke 23 Nov 2026, not built yet): keep the main worlds run by the admin; no player-run worlds. Societies are player groups inside the main world (a name, members, maybe a leader, a shared camp, storage and land). Build them after storage and shelter, since they need those (and trading).
- Campfire claims (Luke's idea, 23 Nov 2026, not built yet; build with societies after storage and shelter): placing a campfire makes a claim round it (Luke: about 50 m; settle whether that's across or the radius: a 50 m radius fits only about 100 claims in a 1 km world, 50 m across about 400). While you're away your person stays near their campfire instead of the main pond. The claim lasts while your person is in the world (the same 3-day away timer) and only while the fire stays lit (Luke, 23 Nov 2026: no grace period). Fuel changes to make that workable (Luke's pick): sticks stay quick fuel (5 min each, for lighting and cooking), a log burns 8 hours, and a fire holds up to 3 days of fuel (9 logs); your person feeds it from their bag while you're away, so spare logs stretch it further. A lapsed claim's fire becomes an ordinary one anyone can take apart; later, storage inside it goes back to the world. Claims can't overlap. A spawn area round the main pond and the start (about 30 m) can't be claimed, so new players always have open land, water and free sticks and stones. Away people stroll round their campfire, walk to the pond to drink when thirsty, then walk back home; in a bigger world, add more ponds and streams (or a water skin) so no claim is too far from water. Society members would share their society's claim.
- Bigger world (Luke, 23 Nov 2026, eventually): worlds of 1 km square. Needs the game to only draw what's near you (its own update). The current 100 m stays exactly as it is; new land gets its own random seed.
