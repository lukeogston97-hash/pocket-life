# Basics: look, clock, age, needs, walking, controls

- Soft low-poly 3D like SEED, minimal UI like One Hour One Life. Luke is very particular about the look: don't restyle without asking.
- One person per player. When you die you start as a new person in the same world.
- Real-time 24-hour clock and real dates, one clock for the whole world (0.1.6): settings.worldTimeZone, Australia/Darwin for the Oceania world (worldNow() in game.js), so day and night are the same for everyone. Regions (decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026): Oceania, Europe, Americas, each its own world on its own time. The world picker sits under Play on the main menu (0.1.7, regions in game-data.json): only Oceania is open; tapping Europe or Americas shows this world's view at that region's time of day (worldNow(tz), menuRegion) with Play saying '<Region> is coming soon'. A world that isn't open yet is drawn fresh (0.2.1, Luke): every tree standing, bushes full, grass grown, no items, campfires or people (setFresh in screens.js, freshLook in core.js; drawing only). Later each will be its own Durable Object world.
- The game doesn't say whose clock that is (Luke, 25 Nov 2026). The audit noted that someone in Sydney may think the clock is wrong, and Claude added "It runs on Darwin time" to the Menu without being asked. Luke had it removed. Don't add words about Darwin time, or other words players see that he hasn't asked for: ask first.
- Age is chosen when making a person (planned: 13 to 90, was 18 to 90) and goes up in real time, a year per real year. Male or female, male is the default with short hair.
- Players are 13+. Deaths: hunger, thirst, tiredness, old age, animals, accidents. No PvP.
- Home storage can't be taken, but if a player is gone too long and upkeep lapses, it goes out to the world.
- Start-from-nothing world. Every item has a weight (stick 1, stone 1). Carry capacity 30.
- Needs are realistic (0.0.7): hunger lasts about a day, thirst about 16 hours awake. Berries are picked one at a time from berry bushes into your inventory and eaten one at a time (10 hunger each). A full bush holds 5, and picked berries grow back one by one (a bare bush is full again after regrowHours, 4). One drink at the pond gives 15 thirst. No water for about 3 days or no food for about a week and you die. About 30 berry bushes (berryBushes.extra in game-data.json adds them in the wider world on their own random numbers).
- Your person walks round rocks, bushes, logs, stumps, tree trunks and the pond: walks find a route on a half-metre grid (A*, findPath in game.js) and cut corners where the line is clear; stuck for 1.5 s means one new route, then "Can't get there". People strolling while away follow the same routes on screen.
- Controls (same on phone and computer): tap the ground for a small menu with Walk here (a faint ring shows where); a quick double-tap walks straight there. Tap or click a stick, stone, berry bush or the pond to open a small menu (Pick up stick, Pick berries, Drink, Walk here); right-click or holding a finger down opens it too. A walk joins the end of the task queue, so it never interrupts anything. A small list above the bottom bar shows what your person is doing now and what's next (hidden when there's nothing to do), with an x on each task and Stop all. UI style (0.0.9, Luke's pick): dark glass like SEED, as little on screen as possible, icons only. Info card top left (name · age, date and time, online dot), Menu round button top right, needs as rings with icons bottom left (tap for names and numbers; a low need pulses red, under 10% it flashes), round Say and Inventory (bag with a weight badge) buttons bottom right (the Craft hammer between them went in 0.1.8: Craft is in the inventory). Say opens a chat bar along the bottom (kept above the keyboard with visualViewport) with the last 6 lines above it; it stays open after sending and closes when you tap the world or press Escape; the bottom buttons hide while chatting. All pop-ups, sheets, tap menus, labels and the Make your person sheet use the same glass (style.css has the theme at the top). Don't restyle without asking. Main menu (0.1.3): the first screen, with Play (shows 'as Name · age' or 'Make your person'), What's new, Settings (Graphics) and Feedback over a live view of the world, the camera slowly circling the main pond (menuCamera). The menu joins the world as a watcher (hello with watch: true; no person, not counted as playing, its messages ignored except feedback); Play sends a second hello on the same connection. Escape, or the Menu button, opens the pause menu: Resume, Exit (back to the main menu, your person goes away; server leave()) and Admin for the admin. Make your person has a Back button. There's no New person: you get a new person when yours dies or leaves the world.  On a computer, W A S D move the camera, Q and E turn it round your person, and R and F tilt it.
- While testing, settings.autoName fills in a name from testNames on the Make your person screen.
- Names are one of a kind (Luke, 25 Nov 2026, 0.1.12): a new person can't take a name a person in the world already has, whether they're playing, away or asleep (Luke picked away keeping theirs). Capitals, extra spaces and look-alike forms of the same letters don't count ("Luke" and " luke " are the same). The names of the dead, people gone after too long away, people who left the world and banned players are free again. Make your person says "Someone in the world already has that name. Try another one." The server decides (nameTaken in server/src/persons.js, the `create` action), so two people picking a name at once can't both get it. The admin's Rename refuses one too. People who already shared a name before this keep it (the admin can rename one). The checks give each page its own ready-made name (tools/tests/lib.py). Checks: cheats.mjs F, admin_extras.mjs D, `menu`.
- 0.3.0 tidy-ups (Luke approved the 23 Nov 2026 audit): age is 13 to 90 (looks.age.min 13), matching players 13+. settings.autoName is off in the real game, so players type their own name (the checks switch it on for themselves). One name for each thing: "Pick berries" (the source's action) and "Too heavy" (the weight limit) everywhere. Panels you only look at (Inventory, Craft, Settings, What's new, Admin and its item picker) close with a Close link at the top right; pop-ups that ask you something keep their own buttons (Resume, Carry on, Send, Cancel). The pause menu has What's new, Settings and Feedback as the same three tiles as the main menu. On a computer (wide screen with a mouse) the corner cards and buttons are drawn 1.2 times bigger. In Craft, a recipe you can't make yet keeps its button shape with fainter words. With a finger, admin chips and buttons are at least 38 px tall.
- A phone held sideways (0.1.10): on a short, wide screen (landscape, under 560 px tall) Make your person puts its sheet down the right, scrolling if it has to, with your person on the left (style.css; renderCreator in game/people.js draws the stage beside the sheet). Before, the bottom of the sheet and Start my life were off the screen, so the game couldn't be started sideways. The inventory gets more of the height there too. The same look, only rearranged. The `sideways` check covers it.
