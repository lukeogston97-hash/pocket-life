# Needs, crafting, chopping, tall grass, held tools

- Planned next (decided with Luke 21 Nov 2026, build in small updates):
  - Crafting (built 0.0.9): the Craft (hammer) button at the bottom right opens its own sheet of recipe cards: what it makes, chips showing what it needs and how many you have (green when enough), and Make (or Need more); Make is checked by the server ('craft'). Recipes in game-data.json, copied to server/game.json. First recipe: stone axe = 2 sticks + 3 stones. Tools (items with tool.uses) show in your hand and have a wear bar; the server keeps wear per person (p.wear). Simple now, more complex later.
  - Chopping (built 0.1.2, rules in chopping in game-data.json): with a stone axe tap a tree for Chop tree (10 s: 1 log + 2 sticks fall, a stump is left, it grows back after regrowDays 3); tap a stump for Remove stump (10 s: 1 log, then the tree is gone for good and no longer blocks walking); tap a log for Chop into sticks (8 s: 5 sticks, was 4 until 0.2.3; weights 0.2.3: log 5, campfire 15, twine and berry 1). Each chop wears the axe (20 uses) and it breaks with a message. Trees are numbered in the order they're laid out (trees[] in game.js, recorded from put() while each tree is placed), the server keeps world.trees {index: [stump|gone, since]}. The old stumps and fallen logs that were part of the world were taken out in 0.2.8 (Luke: stumps only come from chopped trees, and they grow back; dug-up stumps are gone for good; grow-back timing to be looked at with all regrow times later). They're still worked out in nature.js (skipPut, so the same random numbers are used) and their spots kept while the rest is laid out, then freed in start.js (noLongerHere), so nothing else moved (layout baseline: only oldStumps and serverLayout's open ground changed). Taps find the exact tree or stump from the instanced copy that was hit (copyOwner). Logs weigh 10 and aren't drawn in your hand. Open the game with ?debug to reach window.__pl in tests.
  - Rest (built 0.1.1, the moon ring between Health and Hunger): perHour -6.25 awake, whileAsleep +12.5; tap yourself for Sleep / Wake up; any task wakes you; sleeping people lie down with 'sleeping' under their name; away and autopilot people sleep when Rest < away.sleepBelow (35) until full (night-time sleeping can come later). Rest running out doesn't kill (0.1.5, Luke's pick): you collapse asleep where you stand (collapseAtZero, server collapse(), p.collapsed) and can't wake until Rest is back to wakeAt (25); others just see 'sleeping'. Food: hunger empties in about a day and no food kills in about a week (Luke: keep it, it brings people back daily).
  - Stamina (the yellow bolt ring on the right; id energy, named Energy until 0.1.6), short-term since 0.1.6: perHour and whileAsleep +400 (full in about 15 min); tasks and walking spend it (energyCosts: chop 15, craft 5, pickup and harvest 2, drink 1, walkPerMetre 0.15; used by game and server). Below slowBelow (20) you're 'Out of breath' and walking and tasks take slowBy (1.5) times as long (slowness() in game.js; walks send sp so others see the slower walk). Never stops you, not deadly. Health heals 4 an hour (8 asleep) when nothing is empty.
  - Sleeping also slows hunger (-2.5/h) and thirst (-3/h) via whileAsleep.
  - Tall grass and the knife (0.1.6, Luke's idea): plants in game-data.json (tallGrass: 40 clumps, 12 near the start, own seed so nothing else moves; Cut grass gives 1 twine, 6 s by hand or 2 s with a knife; a cut clump drops to stubble and grows back at a random 1 to 4 hours; server keeps world.plants {kind: {i: readyAt}}, 'cut' message, 'plant'/'cutNo' replies). Stone knife = 1 stick + 1 stone + 1 twine, 20 uses (was 10); picks 2 berries at a time (sources.berryBush tool/toolHarvest). Stone axe now also needs 1 twine. Tools wear through wearTool() on the server.
