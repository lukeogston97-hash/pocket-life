## Stone and mining
- Loose stones are picked up by hand and still top up as a safety net. Rocks need the stone pickaxe: Break rock gives 5 stones and the rock is gone, but one broken rock comes back (same spot, beside its boulder) every mining.rockBackHours (4). Boulders are permanent and can't be removed: Mine boulder gives 3 to 5 stones, then it looks worn down and can be mined again after regrowDays (1 day since 0.1.16, the timing pass above). Which rocks are boulders comes from the layout (code.md, Guards).

## Fishing and cooking
- Fish is the first food after berries (Luke, 23 Nov 2026: no wheat or bread). Tap the pond for Fish, with a fishing spear on the tool bar (20 uses, wears each try). A try takes 8 s, catches a fish 7 times in 10 (chance 0.7 since 0.2.6; it was half the time) and costs 3 stamina. An empty pond, or no room for a catch, takes no try: no use of the spear, no stamina (0.2.6; it used to wear the spear). The pond holds 10 fish for everyone (the server keeps w.pond { fish, at }; one comes back every 60 minutes, worked out when someone fishes; Regrow everything refills it). The game doesn't know the count: the server answers 'fished' (caught, Nothing bites, No fish left, Too heavy). People away don't fish.
- Raw fish can't be eaten. A recipe with near campfire and seconds (10 since 0.1.16) makes cooked fish (50 food since 0.2.6, was 35; a berry gives 10); a recipe with seconds uses its things up when it finishes. Fish has its own shape (SHAPES.fish).
- The walk to the pond's edge always ends on dry ground walking allows (pondShape at least 0.9), so drinking and fishing never get stuck with Can't get there.

## Balance pass (Luke, 27 Nov 2026, 0.2.6)

What stood out is below, with what Luke picked. The rest is in later.md (farming, Remove stump, the pickaxe, bigger buildings).
