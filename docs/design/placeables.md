# Placeables: campfires, fuel, sitting

- Placeables (built 0.2.0, the campfire first; decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026): a generic system for things you put down and build. Data: placeables in game-data.json (buildSeconds, buildVerb, solid, optional fire and seats); the item that places one has places: <id> and shows a Place button in the Inventory (it still shows in Craft and the admin Add list by itself, like any item). Placing: a ghost and a green or red ring follow taps on the ground, with a Cancel / Place bar at the bottom; your person walks there, puts it down as an unbuilt site, then builds it (pieces move from a pile into place as the progress). An unbuilt site can be picked back up. Server: world.placed [{ id, kind, x, z, rot, built, fuel, lit, at }] (placed.js), actions place, build, unplace, fire, sit. Game: game/placeables.js (after moving), which adds its tasks to TASK_KINDS in moving.js. Placed things are solid to walk round (addSpot).
  - Stamina (0.2.2, Luke: ran out after a couple of minutes of crafting and chopping): perHour 600 (full in 10 min), whileAsleep 1500, whileSitting 2400 (sitting by a fire, client you.sit and server p.sit), chop costs 10 (was 15). Each session should allow a bit of play.
  - Campfire fuel (0.2.3): otherFuels { log: 1800 } (a log burns 30 min), maxFuel 24 (2 hours); Add log in the tap menu, fire message carries fuel kind.
  - Campfire: 1 log + 3 sticks + 4 stones (stones added 0.2.1, Luke); since 0.1.8 1 log + 2 twigs + 2 stones (recipes as chains), weight 9 since 0.1.9 (its parts; was 12). Seats 1.7 m from the middle (was 1.25, feet ended up in the fire). Fuel is sticks: 5 minutes each (secondsEach 300), holds up to 10 (50 minutes), starts with none (the recipe's sticks go into building it), needs at least 1 stick to light. Fuel only burns while lit, keeps burning while players are away and goes out by itself (lazy: fuel ms as of at, no ticking). Tap menu: Light fire / Put out fire, Add 1 stick, Add n sticks, Sit by the fire (while lit), Inspect. The teepee of sticks shows how much fuel is in it; flames and one warm light (the nearest lit fire) while lit.
  - Sitting (0.2.8, Luke's pick): 6 seats round the fire, one tap, no time to pick. Your person sits until you tap Stand up, give another task (it stands them up and starts straight away, queueTask), or the fire goes out (the game checks each frame; the server's away plan ends at fireOutAt). A queued task; the server keeps p.sit { fire, seat, until } with until a year off (SIT_OPEN) so the person stays sitting while away (away.js plan kind 'sit') and the game carries on sitting when you come back. Tap menu: Sit by the fire (while lit). Tap yourself for Stand up; Stop all also stands you up.
- How a placed thing looks comes from the data (0.1.10 test 6, from the audit: "before the second placed thing, the look has to come from the data"). Until then makePlacedView and the placing ghost both called campfireModel, so anything placed would have been drawn as a campfire. Now placedModel(item) in game/placeables.js picks a builder by the item's look.shape (PLACED_SHAPES; the campfire's is campfireModel). A builder gives { model, R, pieces, fuel, burns }: pieces move from a pile into place as it's built, fuel are the pieces that show how full a fire is, burns go black once it has burnt. A shape with no builder is drawn as its item, set down whole. Nothing a player can see changed: the campfire is built from the same 13 pieces, with the same 5 fuel sticks. The check placed_kinds adds a drying rack from data alone (in the check only, not in the game) and places and builds it next to a campfire.
- Placing grid (0.1.17, Luke 26 Nov 2026): placed things go on an invisible grid of half-metre squares (PLACE_GRID in rules.js, shared with the server, which snaps every place and admin move itself). Each placeable has size in squares ([across, deep], 2 x 2 if left out; the campfire is 2 x 2, 1 m) and faces one of 4 ways: a Turn button (icon) on the place bar turns it a quarter. The placing outline is now the square it takes. Things can go side by side; keepClear keeps others that many metres from its square (campfire 1.5, room for its seats). Old campfires from before stay where they were.
  - Flat ground: the ground under each placed thing is levelled (land.js setFlats: flat out to 1.4 m past its edges, blending back by 2.8 m, since the land's triangles are 2 m). Every game works it out the same from the placed list, oldest first, so a new thing next to an old one takes the old one's level and never tilts it; it goes back when the thing is removed. Trees, rocks, bushes and grass clumps keep the ground they stand on (landMayChange), the pond's bank is never changed, and loose items and grass move with the ground. Spots where the ground rises and falls more than settings.placeMaxRise (0.5 m) say "Too steep here". Luke worried about hills getting choppy; the world itself was left as it is (most 3 m patches rise under 0.5 m).
