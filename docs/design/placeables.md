# Placeables: campfires, fuel, sitting

- Placeables (built 0.2.0, the campfire first; decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026): a generic system for things you put down and build. Data: placeables in game-data.json (buildSeconds, buildVerb, solid, optional fire and seats); the item that places one has places: <id> and shows a Place button in the Inventory (it still shows in Craft and the admin Add list by itself, like any item). Placing: a ghost and a green or red ring follow taps on the ground, with a Cancel / Place bar at the bottom; your person walks there, puts it down as an unbuilt site, then builds it (pieces move from a pile into place as the progress). An unbuilt site can be picked back up. Server: world.placed [{ id, kind, x, z, rot, built, fuel, lit, at }] (placed.js), actions place, build, unplace, fire, sit. Game: game/placeables.js (after moving), which adds its tasks to TASK_KINDS in moving.js. Placed things are solid to walk round (addSpot).
  - Stamina (0.2.2, Luke: ran out after a couple of minutes of crafting and chopping): perHour 600 (full in 10 min), whileAsleep 1500, whileSitting 2400 (sitting by a fire, client you.sit and server p.sit), chop costs 10 (was 15). Each session should allow a bit of play.
  - Campfire fuel (0.2.3): otherFuels { log: 1800 } (a log burns 30 min), maxFuel 24 (2 hours); Add log in the tap menu, fire message carries fuel kind.
  - Campfire: 1 log + 3 sticks + 4 stones (stones added 0.2.1, Luke), weight 17. Seats 1.7 m from the middle (was 1.25, feet ended up in the fire). Fuel is sticks: 5 minutes each (secondsEach 300), holds up to 10 (50 minutes), starts with none (the recipe's sticks go into building it), needs at least 1 stick to light. Fuel only burns while lit, keeps burning while players are away and goes out by itself (lazy: fuel ms as of at, no ticking). Tap menu: Light fire / Put out fire, Add stick, Add n sticks, Sit by campfire (while lit), Inspect. The teepee of sticks shows how much fuel is in it; flames and one warm light (the nearest lit fire) while lit.
  - Sitting (0.2.8, Luke's pick): 6 seats round the fire, one tap, no time to pick. Your person sits until you tap Stand up, give another task (it stands them up and starts straight away, queueTask), or the fire goes out (the game checks each frame; the server's away plan ends at fireOutAt). A queued task; the server keeps p.sit { fire, seat, until } with until a year off (SIT_OPEN) so the person stays sitting while away (away.js plan kind 'sit') and the game carries on sitting when you come back. Tap menu: Sit by campfire (while lit). Tap yourself for Stand up; Stop all also stands you up.
