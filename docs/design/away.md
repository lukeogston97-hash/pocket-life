# Away from the game: timing, sleep and death

- Away from the game (built in 0.0.7, run by the server): your person stays in the world 24/7. For the first 24 hours away they keep themselves going: stroll within away.wanderMax (25 m) of the main pond (away.near), eat from their inventory, pick berries from nearby bushes, and walk to the pond to drink. If they still run out of food or water they can die (water is always there; add more berry bushes if too many die). After 24 hours away they lie down asleep on the ground (needs pause). After another day asleep (away.goneAfterDays 1, so 48 hours away in all, Luke's pick 0.1.4) they're gone and their inventory drops where they slept; Welcome back says how long you have. Coming back shows a Welcome back card. Others see 'away' or 'away, asleep' under the name; sleepers lie down. A grave marks where someone died for a day. Admin extras: tap a person for Fill their needs, Wake up, Send to sleep, Remove from world; tap yourself for Fill my needs; Speed up time (60x for 10 min) in the Admin panel; delete feedback and reports. The Menu's What's new shows devlog from game-data.json (one entry per version, see docs/design/working.md). No offline play: without the server the game shows Joining or Reconnecting. When the game is hidden (another app, phone locked) it says bye and closes, so the person goes away at once; it pings every 25 s and the server closes connections quiet for 90 s. The server ticks every 20 s. Others' actions (bend, pick, kneel to drink) are shown via 'act' messages; away people's strolls carry 'then' so watchers see them drink or pick on arrival. Admin options in tap menus sit behind one 'Admin' item (a submenu with Back). Autopilot (0.0.8 patch, in the game, not the server): in the game but no taps for away.idleMinutes (3) and nothing queued, your person eats, drinks, picks berries and strolls near where you left off, using the normal task queue; an 'On autopilot' chip shows and any tap takes over. Strollers seen on screen follow routes round rocks and the pond (findPath): with a route they keep strictly to it (no sidestepping), without one they retry every second, they never start inside a solid (outsideSolid), and stroll messages carry ms (time to arrive) so their game speeds them up (up to 2.5x) to keep in step with the server (0.1.7). The strolling check in tools/tests watches this.
- Your task list carries on (Luke, 24 Nov 2026, built 0.1.7: "Player task should continue, when the pause or leave the game"):
  - The Menu, the Camp panel and What's new no longer stop your person: they keep walking and working behind it (only taps wait). Maintenance still stops everything.
  - Leaving (Exit, closing the game, or the phone locked for more than away.comeBackSeconds): the server carries on with what's left of the list, every kind of task (Luke picked all of them at once). Each is done the way the game does it, with the same rules (camps, tools, weight, reach); something that has gone is skipped.
  - Hungry or thirsty: they eat and drink as usual, then carry on (Luke's pick). Tired: sleep until rested, then carry on. Night alone doesn't stop the list; once it's done they go to bed as usual (Claude's call; say if night should stop it). The long sleep after 24 hours away stops it; what's left waits.
  - Sitting by the fire as a task holds the rest of the list until the fire goes out, the same as in the game. Autopilot's own little jobs aren't sent (the away routine does those).
  - Sitting while away (0.2.1, Luke's bug, 27 Nov 2026: left sitting by the fire overnight, he came back to his person collapsed with Rest 0 and couldn't wake them): needs come first. Every 15 minutes (SIT_CHECK in away.js) they check, and get up from the fire for bed (night once the list is done, or Rest under away.sleepBelow), a drink or berries, then carry on with the usual away routine (they don't go back to the fire). Running out of Rest while sitting (playing) also stands them up as they collapse. Check: tasks.mjs G.
  - Coming back: what's left is back on your list where the server has you, and Welcome back says how many things on your task list got done.
  - A schedule (doing things at set times) is planned as its own thing, separate from the task list you set (Luke, 24 Nov 2026). The server's task runner (server/src/tasks.js) is built so a schedule can hand it tasks too.
  - How it's built: the game sends { t: 'tasks', list } 3 seconds after the list changes (only if it did) and straight away when leaving (moving.js sendTasks; others.js on hidden, screens.js toMenu). The server keeps it as p.tasks (tasks.js cleanTasks: only real kinds and fields). Away, decide (away.js) calls taskStep after drinking and berries: a walk at walking speed, then a 'work' plan for the task's seconds (game.json now has sources, plants and recipes seconds), then finishTask runs the same actions.js handler a player's message would (actAs). Placing adds the build next, as the game does. hello sends what's left back (tasksBack) with the welcome. Admin: a person's page shows their task list, with Clear task list. Checks: tools/tests/tasks.mjs and the tasks_away browser check.
- Sleeping in your camp's shelter (0.2.5, shelter.md): going to bed while away (night, or tired), your person walks over to a basic shelter in your camp with room in it and sleeps in it, twice as well; the long sleep after a day away is in it too. No shelter, or no room: on the ground, as before.
- Timing, away, sleep and death (decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026, built in 0.1.9 all at once; Luke wants realism in timing):
  - Away: gone for good after 7 days away (Luke, 25 Nov 2026, 0.1.14; was 3 days, and 48 h before that). Sleepers stay visible, lying asleep, until then.
  - While away and asleep (after the first 24 h), hunger and thirst keep dropping slowly (at the asleep rates) but stop at a 10% floor, so away people can't die; they wake hungry and thirsty.
  - Sleep gets a bonus at night (Darwin clock): sleep any time, but Rest fills faster at night. Away and autopilot people go to sleep when it gets dark.
  - Clock stays real Darwin time. Ageing is real time: age goes up 1 each real year on the person's birthday (the day they were made). Old age death left out for now.
  - How it's built: away.goneAfterDays 6 (after sleepAfterHours 24; was 2 until 0.1.14) and away.asleepFloor 10 (advanceStats floor: needs with atZero stop there or where they were, no harm, no death; caught up lazily when they come back). night {from 20, to 6} in game-data.json plus settings.worldTimeZone (server: isNight, morningAfter, hourAt in needs.js); rest.whileAsleepAtNight 16 (full in about 6 h). Away and autopilot people sleep when night starts until morning. ageOf(p) in needs.js: p.age plus birthdays since p.born (Darwin date); sent in me, pub look, yours and died; the game shows Happy birthday.
