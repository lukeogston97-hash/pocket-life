# Away from the game: timing, sleep and death

- Away from the game (built in 0.0.7, run by the server): your person stays in the world 24/7. For the first 24 hours away they keep themselves going: stroll within away.wanderMax (25 m) of the main pond (away.near), eat from their inventory, pick berries from nearby bushes, and walk to the pond to drink. If they still run out of food or water they can die (water is always there; add more berry bushes if too many die). After 24 hours away they lie down asleep on the ground (needs pause). After another day asleep (away.goneAfterDays 1, so 48 hours away in all, Luke's pick 0.1.4) they're gone and their inventory drops where they slept; Welcome back says how long you have. Coming back shows a Welcome back card. Others see 'away' or 'away · fast asleep' under the name; sleepers lie down. A grave marks where someone died for a day. Admin extras: tap a person for Fill their needs, Wake up, Send to sleep, Remove from world; tap yourself for Fill my needs; Speed up time (60x for 10 min) in the Admin panel; delete feedback and reports. The Menu's What's new shows devlog from game-data.json (add an entry for every version). No offline play: without the server the game shows Joining or Reconnecting. When the game is hidden (another app, phone locked) it says bye and closes, so the person goes away at once; it pings every 25 s and the server closes connections quiet for 90 s. The server ticks every 20 s. Others' actions (bend, pick, kneel to drink) are shown via 'act' messages; away people's strolls carry 'then' so watchers see them drink or pick on arrival. Admin options in tap menus sit behind one 'Admin' item (a submenu with Back). Autopilot (0.0.8 patch, in the game, not the server): in the game but no taps for away.idleMinutes (3) and nothing queued, your person eats, drinks, picks berries and strolls near where you left off, using the normal task queue; an 'On autopilot' chip shows and any tap takes over. Strollers seen on screen follow routes round rocks and the pond (findPath): with a route they keep strictly to it (no sidestepping), without one they retry every second, they never start inside a solid (outsideSolid), and stroll messages carry ms (time to arrive) so their game speeds them up (up to 2.5x) to keep in step with the server (0.1.7). The strolling check in tools/tests watches this.
- Timing, away, sleep and death (decided with Luke 22 Nov 2026, built in 0.1.9 all at once; Luke wants realism in timing):
  - Away: gone for good after 3 days away (was 48 h). Sleepers stay visible, lying asleep, until then.
  - While away and asleep (after the first 24 h), hunger and thirst keep dropping slowly (at the asleep rates) but stop at a 10% floor, so away people can't die; they wake hungry and thirsty.
  - Sleep gets a bonus at night (Darwin clock): sleep any time, but Rest fills faster at night. Away and autopilot people go to sleep when it gets dark.
  - Clock stays real Darwin time. Ageing is real time: age goes up 1 each real year on the person's birthday (the day they were made). Old age death left out for now.
  - How it's built: away.goneAfterDays 2 (after sleepAfterHours 24) and away.asleepFloor 10 (advanceStats floor: needs with atZero stop there or where they were, no harm, no death; caught up lazily when they come back). night {from 20, to 6} in game-data.json plus settings.worldTimeZone (server: isNight, morningAfter, hourAt in needs.js); rest.whileAsleepAtNight 16 (full in about 6 h). Away and autopilot people sleep when night starts until morning. ageOf(p) in needs.js: p.age plus birthdays since p.born (Darwin date); sent in me, pub look, yours and died; the game shows Happy birthday.
