# Claims: your campfire claims the land round it

- Built in 0.3.0 (decided with Luke 23 Nov 2026, one question at a time). Rules in game-data.json "claims" (radius 10, spawnRadius 16, placeable campfire, ringShowsWithin 25); the server decides everything (server/src/claims.js), the game shows it (the end of game/placeables.js).
- Your first campfire becomes your claim when you light it: 10 m round the fire (Luke's pick for today's 100 m world; raise claims.radius when the world grows. A 10 m claim fits a fire, 2 or 3 shelters, a few chests and a small farm). It is yours only while the fire burns: no grace period. Once it's out (fuel ran out, or you put it out) the land is free and the cold fire is anyone's to pack up or take apart. Lighting it again claims again, if the spot is still free.
- One claim each. A second fire you light is just a fire. You can end your own claim by putting the fire out (then pack it up or take it apart).
- Where a claim can't go: overlapping another claim (fires under 2 x radius apart), or reaching into the free area round the main pond and the start (claims.spawnRadius 16 m from the pond's middle, about the size of a claim round the water's edge). A fire lit there is just a fire. Anyone can still place and build things in that free area. The rule is claimProblem in game/rules.js (shared with the server).
- Inside someone else's claim others can only walk through. Stopped (by the server, with "This is <name>'s claim"): placing or building, picking up loose items, chopping, mining, picking berries, cutting grass, and using their fire at all (light, put out, add fuel, sit, cook, pack up, take apart). The game's tap menus show "<name>'s claim · Only they can use things here" with just Walk here (menuActions in input.js), placing there is refused, and autopilot and people away leave berry bushes in other people's claims alone. The admin can do anything anywhere.
- Fuel, so a fire can last (Luke's pick): sticks stay quick fuel (5 min each), a log burns 8 hours, and a fire holds up to 3 days (fire.maxHours 72), the same as the 3-day away timer.
- How players see it: a faint ring on the ground at the edge (green for yours, white for others; shown within ringShowsWithin metres), a small tag when you walk in ("Your claim" or "<name>'s claim"), the fire's tap menu title says whose claim it is and how long the fuel lasts, and while placing a campfire a ring shows the claim it would make, with a line saying if it can't claim there and why.
- While you're away: your person strolls within their claim instead of by the pond, puts sticks and logs from their bag on the fire when there's room (logs first; they wait for half an hour of room so they don't walk over for every stick), and still walks to the pond to drink, then back. Welcome back says they kept the fire going.
- Fires lit before 0.3.0 are just fires until they're lit again.
- Societies (0.1.4, societies.md): a society has one claim, its leader's lit campfire (th.claim 's:<id>'); members use it as their own.
- Not built yet: storage inside a claim, a claim marker other than the fire, more water for a bigger world (see later.md).
