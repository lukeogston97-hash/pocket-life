# Online: one shared world, chat, safety, the first admin tools

- Online (started in 0.0.6): one world for everyone. Both links join the same world (the live one): the test link is only for trying a new build in the real world before everyone gets it. Keep test builds' world layout the same as the release, or shared items and bushes won't line up. Other people show with a name tag and walk about; the Say button (or Enter) shows a speech bubble over your head. Sticks, stones and berries are shared: the server holds them, picking up or harvesting asks the server (if someone beat you to it, it comes back out of your inventory), and drops go through the server. Online, the world tops up one stick and one stone every respawnMinutes (10) while there are fewer on the ground than startInWorld: sticks under trees, stones by boulders (later this slows down once sticks come from trees and stones from rocks). The first player into a new world sends its starting layout (seed). Your person, needs and inventory live on the server (0.0.7), tied to the phone's random id (no sign-in yet). The HUD shows a dot (green online, orange connecting, grey offline) and how many are online. The Menu has Graphics (Low, Medium, High, saved on the device) and Send feedback (stored on the server; read it in Admin, Feedback, reports, players). Chat safety: bad words in speech bubbles show as stars, and a bad name gets 'Pick a different name' (chatFilter in game-data.json; tools/worlds.py test-build copies it to server/words.json for the server). Tap another person for Mute (hides their bubbles, saved on the phone), Report (reason plus note, stored on the server with what they said lately) and Walk here. Names also can't contain admin, mod and similar (chatFilter.names). The server limits how fast each player can chat, pick up, harvest, drop, send feedback and report (LIMITS in server/src/shared.js); too fast gets 'Slow down a little'. Each phone has a random id; others only see a scrambled tag of it. Decorative pebbles, flint and the coloured flowers are no longer drawn (still laid out, so nothing else moves) because they looked like stones you could pick up. Admin player: typing /admin and the code in the Say box (never said out loud) makes that phone the admin, remembered on the phone (/admin off forgets it). The code is the Cloudflare secret ADMIN_CODE that Luke set; it's never in the repo. The Menu then has Admin: maintenance on or off with a message (release-link players see Back soon; the test link and the admin still get in), message everyone (a banner), Feedback, reports, players (tabs: Feedback, Reports, Online, Banned), admin tag on or off (shows 'Name · Admin'), and Reset the world (tap twice; everyone rejoins and the first lays out the starting layout). Tapping the ground, a person or an item as admin adds an Admin item (Add <item>, Teleport here; Mute for everyone, Kick, Ban, Teleport to them; Remove). Next: trading (both players accept). No direct giving for now.

## Your person on other devices, and leaving for good
- Play on other devices (built 0.1.12, 25 Nov 2026; Luke picked link codes over Google sign-in, username and password, or email): Settings > Play on other devices. Get a code (6 letters, 10 minutes, used once) on the device with the person and type it on the other device. That device's own guest person is removed after a warning. Unlink this device makes it a new guest. The admin's player page shows how many devices and has Unlink all devices. Losing every linked device means losing the person. Server: server/src/accounts.js (whoFor maps a device to its person); checks accounts.mjs and `leaving`. Google sign-in may come later, optional (docs/design/later.md).
- Leave the world for good (built 0.1.11, 25 Nov 2026): Settings, a warning ("Leave the world for good? Mia and everything about you will be removed. This can't be undone."), then hold a button for 2 seconds. Their things drop where they stand (like dying; they fade after 7 days). It removes their person, past people, device links and feedback (feedback keeps the phone's scrambled id so it can be found). Their camp follows the usual leaving rules. A ban stays. The admin's Log notes that someone left (no name). Server: persons.js forget, the 'forget' action; game: screens.js openLeave; check forget.mjs.
