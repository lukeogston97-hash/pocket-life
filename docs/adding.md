# Adding things to Pocket Life

The short way to add each kind of thing. Most are one edit to a file in content/: items.json (items, itemGroups, retired), recipes.json, placeables.json (placeables, crops), world.json (sources, plants, chopping, mining, berryBushes, regions, daylight, night), rules.json (settings, stats, energyCosts, away, claims, societies, liveSettings), people.json, text.json (tips, chatFilter) and version.json (version, build, devlog). Each file's "_help" explains its sections. After any change in content/, run `python3 tools/worlds.py test-build`: it makes test/game-data.json (never edit that) and the server's copies.

## Content (content/*.json only)
- **Item** (content/items.json): copy an entry in `items`, give it a new id (lower case, no spaces), name, plural, weight (whole number), look and a short "about". It shows up by itself in the Inventory, Recipes, the admin Add item picker and Give items, under a heading: Tools (has tool.uses), Food (has eat), Building (has places) or Materials, unless it has a "group" (one of `itemGroups`: Materials, Parts, Tools, Food, Building, Clothing, Decoration, Misc). Each heading shows once something is under it; add one to `itemGroups` for a new heading (lint checks every group is in the list).
- **Taking an item out** (like twigs, 0.1.21): remove it from `items` and from every recipe and fuel that uses it, then add it to `retired` with what any left over become (another item's id, or null to just take them away). When the world starts, the server turns any still in an inventory or on the ground into that (retireOld in server/src/persons.js; tools/tests/retire.mjs checks it). Keep the entry for a while, so an earlier time restored gets tidied too. Only renaming? Change its name and plural and keep the id (Long stick became Long tool handle, id long-stick).
- **Food**: an item with `eat` (like `{ "hunger": 10 }`). Something that must be cooked first: no `eat` on the raw item, and a recipe with `"near": "campfire"` that makes the cooked one.
- **Tool**: an item with `tool.uses`. To make a source or plant need it, set `tool` there.
- **Recipe**: add to `recipes` (`id`, `needs`, `makes`, optional `keeps` for tools it needs but doesn't use up, optional `group`, optional `near` for a station like the campfire). It shows by itself on the inventory's Recipes tab (greyed out while a player doesn't carry everything it takes), tapping it shows its things on the craft mat above the list, and Make lights up once they carry all of them (needs plus keeps). Keep it guessable: 2 or 3 things, at most 2 of each, big things made from smaller steps. Lint stops two recipes wanting the same things.
- **Something that lies about and respawns** (like sticks and stones): give the item `startInWorld` (how many) and `spawnsNear` (`trees` or `boulders`). It starts turning up by itself, no world reset needed.
- **Cuttable plant** (like tall grass): an entry in `plants`, with its own `seed` so nothing else in the world moves.
- **Placeable** (like the campfire): an entry in `placeables`, plus an item with `"places": "<id>"`. Fires, seats, Pack up, Take apart and the admin's Move, Remove and Fill with fuel all come with it. Its look comes from its item's shape (the item's `look.shape`): with nothing more it's drawn as that item, set down whole, sliding into place as it's built. To have it put together piece by piece, show its fuel or char when burnt, give the shape a builder in PLACED_SHAPES (game/placeables.js; the campfire's is campfireModel in game/items.js).
- **Something that holds things** (like the small wooden crate, 0.2.2): a placeable with `"storage": { "squares": 5 }` (and `inCamp` to keep it to your own camp). Open on its tap menu, its squares at the top of the inventory, Put in and Take, Empty it first before packing up, and the admin's Empty it all come with it (docs/design/storage.md).
- **Placeable dug with a tool** (like the farm field): an entry in `placeables` with `madeWith` (a tool) instead of an item: Dig on the ground's tap menu while carrying it, Flatten in its own menu, `inCamp` to keep it to your own camp, its look from `look.shape` (a builder in PLACED_SHAPES).
- **Crop** (like the berry bush on a farm field): an entry in `crops` (seed item, `on` the placeable it grows on, growHours, plantSeconds, `like` the source it's picked like once grown). Plant shows on the field's tap menu by itself; the admin's Grow now and the live grow time come with it.
- **Fishing at a water source**: `fish` on the source (see `sources.pond.fish`): tool, catch, seconds, chance, holds, regrowMinutes. The server keeps the fish count. A recipe with `seconds` (like cooking) takes that long and uses its things up at the end.
- **A look with no code** (0.2.0): `"look": { "shape": "parts", "parts": [...] }`, pieces (box, cylinder, cone, ball) with size, at, turn and colour (items' _help says how). Pieces of one colour are joined and shared, so lots of them are cheap. Use it for most new items; hand-built shapes are for things that need more (the axe, the campfire).
- **Something you work for things that grows back** (an ore vein, a reed bed, 0.2.0): an entry in `plants` (content/world.json) with its own `seed`, `count`, `gives`, `seconds`, `regrowHours`, `tool` (and `toolNeeded: true` if it can't be done by hand), `area` ({x, z, r}: only there) and `look` (`form: "parts"` with pieces). The tap menu, the task, the server's checks, carrying on while away, and the admin's Grow back (tap it in admin mode) and Regrow everything all come with it. Check it like `ore_vein` in checks.py.
- **A new item shape**: add it to SHAPES in game/items.js, then items can use it by name. If lots of them will lie on the ground, build its geometry with sharedGeo (as sticks, stones and logs do) so they're drawn together and stay cheap on phones.

- **Before pushing**: `python3 tools/tests/lint.py` checks the content for mistakes first (an item name that doesn't exist, a shape that isn't there, a weight that isn't a whole number...) and says what's wrong in plain words.

## Settings and numbers
- **A setting the admin can change live** (Admin > Settings): add it to `liveSettings` in content/rules.json (id, name, group, unit, min, max, step), then wire the id up in two places: RULES in server/src/live.js and LIVE_SET plus LIVE_BASE in game/admin.js.

## Code (when data isn't enough)
- **A rule both the game and the server work out** (how needs move, how a fire burns, what fits where): it goes in test/game/rules.js, once. test-build copies it to the server. Never write the same maths in both places.
- **A new action a player does**: a handler in server/src/actions.js (and a LIMITS entry in shared.js), a task in game/moving.js, a tap menu entry in game/input.js (thingActions). The game sends it with sendAct (online.js), so it survives a short break in the connection. On the server, check the person is near what they're acting on (this.near, this.spotOf in guard.js) and that it isn't in someone else's claim (this.notYours in claims.js). Then give the task its away version in server/src/tasks.js (its fields in cleanTasks, where to stand and how long in taskHow, the message in taskMessage), so your person carries on with it while you're away (0.1.7); tools/tests/tasks.mjs checks each kind.
- **A new admin tool**: a case in server/src/admin.js (every admin message starts with `a.`; only a connection in admin mode gets that far), a line in TOOLS there if it changes something (that's what puts it in the Log), and a button in game/admin.js: on a dashboard tab, or in the menu for a tapped thing (adminThingActions, adminPlacedActions, adminGroundActions, adminPersonActions). A tool that answers in words uses `done(text)`. Add it to tools/tests/admin_extras.mjs (a quick test straight against the server, no browser).
- **A new kind of world thing** (ore vein, animal): needs code for drawing, tapping and its server state, like trees and rocks. Keep the world layout the same: give it its own random seed. For the admin, add it to MOVABLE in server/src/admin.js and movedThing in game/world-build.js. So the server can check players are near it, add its spots to layoutData (game/online.js) and cleanLayout and LISTS (server/src/guard.js). More water (ponds, streams): teach nearWater in guard.js about it.
- **A new action that takes time in the game** (like chopping): in its handler on the server, once everything else about it is fine, `if (!this.worked(ws, me, p, seconds)) { reply({ t: 'slow' }); reply(this.mine(p)); return; }` (guard.js), with the same seconds the game waits. Without it a changed game can do it as fast as it likes.
- **Anything new that puts a thing on the ground** (server): give the item `laid: laidNow()` (shared.js) so it fades with everything else people leave lying about (nature.js fadeOld). If you forget, the hourly look gives it a time anyway, unless it's a kind the world tops up by itself.
- **A new part of the game code**: a new file in test/game/ (an ES module) and its import in game/main.js, below the files it uses. It imports what it needs from files above it; something from a file further down, or a variable another file owns and this one changes, goes through game.name (game/links.js: the owner adds it to its share({...}) at the top). lint.py says if an import goes the wrong way.
- **A change to what's saved** (a field renamed, moved or split up): a step at the end of STEPS in server/src/upgrades.js with the next number, FORMAT set to it, and a check in tools/tests/upgrades.mjs. The live world is upgraded once when the server starts. Steps must be safe to run twice and must never drop what they don't understand.
- **Saving** (each write counts against Cloudflare's free 100,000 a day): when a person changes in a way that lasts (what they carry, eating, drinking, feeding a fire), `this.touch(p)`. For someone away who only walked on or whose needs ticked over, `this.touchLater(p)` (saved about once an hour, since the server can play them forward again). The world: `this.save()` only when something in it changed. After changing what gets saved, run `python3 tools/tests/load.py 300`.
- **Errors**: no need to wrap new code in try/catch. The server's tick and each message, and the game's frame, already catch a problem, carry on and put a note in the admin's Inbox (Server error / Game error). Fix what shows up there.

## The admin grows with the game
Luke's rule (24 Nov 2026): when something new is added, the admin's control for it is added in the same update. Admin mode is docs/design/admin.md.
- **Items, recipes and live settings**: nothing to do. They show up by themselves in Add item, Give items, the Items tab (weight, uses, filling, recipe amounts) and the Settings tab.
- **A new number on an item or recipe that should be changeable live**: add it to `places()` and `tidy()` in server/src/live-items.js and to `itemPlace()` in game/admin.js (its label in the open item, and for an item's own number a `sum`: how the item's line sums it up, like "weighs 2").
- **A new thing in the world** (a placeable, an animal, an ore vein): a menu for the admin when it's tapped (adminActionsFor in game/admin-mode.js), with at least Move and Remove. If it has state worth seeing in one place (like fires and claims), a list on the World tab.
- **A new thing a person has or is** (a skill, a pet, a home): show it on their page (drawPlayer in game/admin.js, playerPage in server/src/admin.js) with a way to change it.
- **Something worth knowing happened** (rare things only, never one line per ordinary action): `this.note('world', text)` puts it in the Log (server/src/activity.js).
- **A new tab**: a button with `data-admin-tab` and a page with `data-admin-page` in test/index.html, and a line in showAdminTab (game/admin.js). The tabs sit in rows of four.
- **Always**: it must work on a computer and on a phone, and only from admin mode (check a player sending the same message gets nothing: tools/tests/admin_extras.mjs does this for the new tools).

## Every update
- Bump `build` in content/version.json (`version` only goes up once per release, see docs/design/working.md), add short lines to the current version's devlog entry there (in "added", "improved" or "fixed", see docs/design/working.md), run `python3 tools/tests/lint.py` and the check for the area you touched, push to main and Luke tries the test link.
- Add the decision to the right file in docs/design/.
- Release only when Luke says: `sh tools/release.sh`, commit, push.
