# Pocket Life (working name)

Luke's mobile "life in your pocket" game: SEED meets One Hour One Life. Built step by step with Claude.

## Design decisions (keep to these)
- Soft low-poly 3D like SEED, minimal UI like One Hour One Life. Luke is very particular about the look: don't restyle without asking.
- One person per player. When you die you start as a new person in the same world.
- Real-time 24-hour clock and real dates (the world runs on the player's real local time, day and night follow it).
- Age is chosen when making a person (planned: 13 to 90, was 18 to 90) and goes up in real time, a year per real year. Male or female, male is the default with short hair.
- Players are 13+. Deaths: hunger, thirst, tiredness, old age, animals, accidents. No PvP.
- Home storage can't be taken, but if a player is gone too long and upkeep lapses, it goes out to the world.
- Start-from-nothing world. Every item has a weight (stick 1, stone 1). Carry capacity 30.
- Needs are realistic (0.0.7): hunger lasts about a day, thirst about 16 hours awake. Berries are picked one at a time from berry bushes into your inventory and eaten one at a time (10 hunger each). A full bush holds 5, and picked berries grow back one by one (a bare bush is full again after regrowHours, 4). One drink at the pond gives 15 thirst. No water for about 3 days or no food for about a week and you die. About 30 berry bushes (berryBushes.extra in game-data.json adds them in the wider world on their own random numbers).
- Your person walks round rocks, bushes, logs, stumps, tree trunks and the pond: walks find a route on a half-metre grid (A*, findPath in game.js) and cut corners where the line is clear; stuck for 1.5 s means one new route, then "Can't get there". People strolling while away follow the same routes on screen.
- Controls (same on phone and computer): tap the ground for a small menu with Walk here (a faint ring shows where); a quick double-tap walks straight there. Tap or click a stick, stone, berry bush or the pond to open a small menu (Pick up Stick, Harvest Berries, Drink, Walk here); right-click or holding a finger down opens it too. A walk joins the end of the task queue, so it never interrupts anything. A small list above the bottom bar shows what your person is doing now and what's next (hidden when there's nothing to do), with an x on each task and Stop all. UI style (0.0.9, Luke's pick): dark glass like SEED, as little on screen as possible, icons only. Info card top left (name · age, date and time, online dot), Menu round button top right, needs as rings with icons bottom left (tap for names and numbers; a low need pulses red, under 10% it flashes), round Say, Craft (hammer) and Inventory (bag with a weight badge) buttons bottom right. Say opens a chat bar along the bottom (kept above the keyboard with visualViewport) with the last 6 lines above it; it stays open after sending and closes when you tap the world or press Escape; the bottom buttons hide while chatting. All pop-ups, sheets, tap menus, labels and the Make your person sheet use the same glass (style.css has the theme at the top). Don't restyle without asking. Main menu (0.1.3): the first screen, with Play (shows 'as Name · age' or 'Make your person'), What's new, Settings (Graphics) and Feedback over a live view of the world, the camera slowly circling the main pond (menuCamera). The menu joins the world as a watcher (hello with watch: true; no person, not counted as playing, its messages ignored except feedback); Play sends a second hello on the same connection. Escape, or the Menu button, opens the pause menu: Resume, Exit (back to the main menu, your person goes away; server leave()) and Admin for the admin. Make your person has a Back button. There's no New person: you get a new person when yours dies or leaves the world.  On a computer, W A S D move the camera, Q and E turn it round your person, and R and F tilt it.
- While testing, settings.autoName fills in a name from testNames on the Make your person screen.
- Online (started in 0.0.6): one world for everyone. Both links join the same world (the live one): the test link is only for trying a new build in the real world before everyone gets it. Keep test builds' world layout the same as the release, or shared items and bushes won't line up. Other people show with a name tag and walk about; the Say button (or Enter) shows a speech bubble over your head. Sticks, stones and berries are shared: the server holds them, picking up or harvesting asks the server (if someone beat you to it, it comes back out of your inventory), and drops go through the server. Online, the world tops up one stick and one stone every respawnMinutes (10) while there are fewer on the ground than startInWorld: sticks under trees, stones by boulders (later this slows down once sticks come from trees and stones from rocks). The first player into a new world sends its starting layout (seed). Your person, needs and inventory live on the server (0.0.7), tied to the phone's random id (no sign-in yet). The HUD shows a dot (green online, orange connecting, grey offline) and how many are online. The Menu has Graphics (Low, Medium, High, saved on the device) and Send feedback (stored on the server; read it in Admin, Feedback, reports, players). Chat safety: bad words in speech bubbles show as stars, and a bad name gets 'Pick a different name' (chatFilter in game-data.json; tools/worlds.py test-build copies it to server/words.json for the server). Tap another person for Mute (hides their bubbles, saved on the phone), Report (reason plus note, stored on the server with what they said lately) and Walk here. Names also can't contain admin, mod and similar (chatFilter.names). The server limits how fast each player can chat, pick up, harvest, drop, send feedback and report (LIMITS in server/src/index.js); too fast gets 'Slow down a little'. Each phone has a random id; others only see a scrambled tag of it. Decorative pebbles, flint and the coloured flowers are no longer drawn (still laid out, so nothing else moves) because they looked like stones you could pick up. Admin player: typing /admin and the code in the Say box (never said out loud) makes that phone the admin, remembered on the phone (/admin off forgets it). The code is the Cloudflare secret ADMIN_CODE that Luke set; it's never in the repo. The Menu then has Admin: maintenance on or off with a message (release-link players see Back soon; the test link and the admin still get in), message everyone (a banner), Feedback, reports, players (tabs: Feedback, Reports, Online, Banned), admin tag on or off (shows 'Name · Admin'), and Reset the world (tap twice; everyone rejoins and the first lays out the starting layout). Tapping the ground, a person or an item as admin adds an Admin item (Add <item>, Teleport here; Mute for everyone, Kick, Ban, Teleport to them; Remove). Next: trading (both players accept). No direct giving for now.
- Away from the game (built in 0.0.7, run by the server): your person stays in the world 24/7. For the first 24 hours away they keep themselves going: stroll within away.wanderMax (25 m) of the main pond (away.near), eat from their inventory, pick berries from nearby bushes, and walk to the pond to drink. If they still run out of food or water they can die (water is always there; add more berry bushes if too many die). After 24 hours away they lie down asleep on the ground (needs pause). After another day asleep (away.goneAfterDays 1, so 48 hours away in all, Luke's pick 0.1.4) they're gone and their inventory drops where they slept; Welcome back says how long you have. Coming back shows a Welcome back card. Others see 'away' or 'asleep' under the name; sleepers lie down. A grave marks where someone died for a day. Admin extras: tap a person for Fill their needs, Wake up, Send to sleep, Remove from world; tap yourself for Fill my needs; Speed up time (60x for 10 min) in the Admin panel; delete feedback and reports. The Menu's What's new shows devlog from game-data.json (add an entry for every version). No offline play: without the server the game shows Joining or Reconnecting. When the game is hidden (another app, phone locked) it says bye and closes, so the person goes away at once; it pings every 25 s and the server closes connections quiet for 90 s. The server ticks every 20 s. Others' actions (bend, pick, kneel to drink) are shown via 'act' messages; away people's strolls carry 'then' so watchers see them drink or pick on arrival. Admin options in tap menus sit behind one 'Admin' item (a submenu with Back). Autopilot (0.0.8 patch, in the game, not the server): in the game but no taps for away.idleMinutes (3) and nothing queued, your person eats, drinks, picks berries and strolls near where you left off, using the normal task queue; an 'On autopilot' chip shows and any tap takes over. Strollers seen on screen follow routes round rocks and the pond (findPath).
- Planned next (decided with Luke 21 Nov 2026, build in small updates):
  - Crafting (built 0.0.9): the Craft (hammer) button at the bottom right opens its own sheet of recipe cards: what it makes, chips showing what it needs and how many you have (green when enough), and Make (or Need more); Make is checked by the server ('craft'). Recipes in game-data.json, copied to server/game.json. First recipe: stone axe = 2 sticks + 3 stones. Tools (items with tool.uses) show in your hand and have a wear bar; the server keeps wear per person (p.wear). Simple now, more complex later.
  - Chopping (built 0.1.2, rules in chopping in game-data.json): with a stone axe tap a tree for Chop tree (10 s: 1 log + 2 sticks fall, a stump is left, it grows back after regrowDays 3); tap a stump for Remove stump (10 s: 1 log, then the tree is gone for good and no longer blocks walking); tap a log for Chop into sticks (8 s: 4 sticks). Each chop wears the axe (20 uses) and it breaks with a message. Trees are numbered in the order they're laid out (trees[] in game.js, recorded from put() while each tree is placed), the server keeps world.trees {index: [stump|gone, since]}. The old stumps already in the world (oldStumps[] in game.js, world.oldStumps on the server) can be dug out the same way, for good. Taps find the exact tree or stump from the instanced copy that was hit (copyOwner). Logs weigh 10 and aren't drawn in your hand. Open the game with ?debug to reach window.__pl in tests.
  - Rest (built 0.1.1, the moon ring between Health and Hunger): perHour -6.25 awake, whileAsleep +12.5; tap yourself for Sleep / Wake up; any task wakes you; sleeping people lie down with 'sleeping' under their name; away and autopilot people sleep when Rest < away.sleepBelow (35) until full (night-time sleeping can come later). Rest running out doesn't kill (0.1.5, Luke's pick): you collapse asleep where you stand (collapseAtZero, server collapse(), p.collapsed) and can't wake until Rest is back to wakeAt (25); others just see 'sleeping'. Food: hunger empties in about a day and no food kills in about a week (Luke: keep it, it brings people back daily).
  - Energy (built 0.1.1, the yellow bolt ring on the right): perHour +30, whileAsleep +60; tasks and walking spend it (energyCosts in game-data.json, used by game and server); too little for a task and your person says they need a breather. Not deadly.
  - Sleeping also slows hunger (-2.5/h) and thirst (-3/h) via whileAsleep.
  - Held tools: the axe uses hold 'axe' (gripped at the handle end, carried head-down along the line of the arm with the blade forward, so raising the arm to chop lifts it overhead), worked out in the person's frame and converted to the hand's frame in drawHeld.
  - No families or babies: everyone starts as an adult person they make.
- Sign-in (decided 21 Nov 2026, not built yet): Google sign-in later, optional, so the same person works on phone and PC; signing in keeps the person you already have. The server's whoFor(m) in index.js is where it plugs in (map 'acct:<google id>' to the phone's who). Luke makes the Google Cloud web client ID when we build it.
- Placeables and buildings: not built yet; when they come they must show in the admin Add list and Craft automatically, like items.
- Later: everything saved on the server, day planner, skills, more stats, regions and time zones, app store wrapper.

## How the code is laid out
- Files (the same set in test/, the root and releases/<version>/): index.html is the page and a small loader; style.css is the look of the menus and buttons; game.js is the game code (three.js r170 from jsDelivr through an import map); game-data.json is all the content. No build step.
- The loader fetches game-data.json fresh, then loads style.css and game.js with its version and build added to their addresses, so phones never mix old and new files.
- game-data.json: all content. Items, recipes, needs (stats), things to use (sources), day and night colours, character looks, body shapes, settings. It has a "help" section explaining each part.
- Adding content should almost always be a small edit to game-data.json, not code. New items automatically appear in the admin's Add list, the Inventory and Craft (with a picture drawn from the 3D model); keep it that way for placeables and buildings when they come.
- The world is settings.worldSize metres square (100 for now), with thick forest past the edge. The clearing round the start is laid out from its own random numbers and the rest of the world from a second set, so changing the world never reshuffles the clearing.
- Keep it light for phones. Lots of copies of a shape are drawn as one InstancedMesh, split into chunks of the map. Grass is only drawn near the camera. Far-off trees use simpler shapes.
- Since 0.0.7 nothing about the game is saved on the phone except settings (graphics, mutes, the admin code) and the phone's random id. The server holds people (one storage key per person, p:<id>), the world, feedback and reports.
- server/ is the online world: a Cloudflare Worker (named pocket-life) with one Durable Object holding the whole world (named live). src/index.js is the world, people and admin; src/people.js is needs and inventory; src/filter.js the chat filter; game.json and words.json are copied from test/game-data.json by tools/worlds.py test-build (so run it after changing needs, items, sources, away or chatFilter). PROTOCOL in index.js and proto in the game's hello must match. It runs at wss://pocket-life.misty-glade-6e8e.workers.dev/world. Cloudflare builds and deploys it from the server folder on every push to main. The game finds it through "server" in game-data.json.
- Cloudflare free plan (Luke is on it, 0.1.4): 100,000 requests a day (20 incoming WebSocket messages count as 1, plus connections and alarms) and 100,000 storage writes a day; past either, things fail until 00:00 UTC (9:30 am Darwin). So: at most settings.maxPlaying (20) play at once, the rest wait in line on the main menu (server line(), callLine(): 'line' with your place, 'turn' when a spot opens, the game then sends hello again); walks are sent once with where they end (move with tx, tz) plus every 2.5 s, and other games walk them along findPath (heardMove); people are saved straight away after doing something (flush(who)) but walking and needs ticking over at most every 5 minutes (SAVE_EVERY); every storage write goes through put() so it's counted. The Admin panel shows today's use (a.state, usageNow). Phones only draw the 30 people nearest the view (SHOW_PEOPLE, pickShown). If it gets busy, the $5 a month Workers Paid plan removes the daily caps.
- If the frame rate drops below about 45, the game lowers its sharpness a step (pixel ratio), never below 1.

## Working on it
- Build in SMALL steps: Luke asked for just a couple of things per update from 0.0.7 on. After each change, tell Luke in a few short lines what changed and what to try on his phone.
- Bump the version with every update (0.0.6, then 0.0.7 and so on). It's only in game-data.json: "version", plus "build" which starts at 1 for each new version and counts up with each test copy. The page shows it, like "v0.0.6 (test 2)".
- To test needs quickly, raise settings.needsSpeed (60 makes an hour pass in a minute). Put it back to 1 before pushing.
- game-data.json is loaded with fetch, so opening index.html straight from the disk won't work. Test with a local server (python3 -m http.server) or the test link.
- Test first, then release. Make every change in the test/ folder, push it to main, and tell Luke to try it on the test link: https://lukeogston97-hash.github.io/pocket-life/test/
- Each new test build: bump "build", then run python3 tools/worlds.py test-build so older test games move to it.
- Maintenance: Luke switches it on and off himself in the game (Admin in the Menu). Release-link players see a Back soon screen, needs pause, and it rejoins by itself. The test link still gets in, so Luke can try the new build first.
- server/worlds.json holds the minVersion for each link. Older games get sent to the newest version (they wait and retry if the link hasn't updated yet).
- Admin: all in the game on Luke's phone (the admin player), not a separate page.
- Only when Luke says to release it ("release", "publish"), run sh tools/release.sh (it also sets the release link's minVersion to version.build, so a patch moves everyone on too; Luke turns maintenance off in the game), then commit and push. It copies test/ to the root (the release link: https://lukeogston97-hash.github.io/pocket-life/) and keeps a copy in releases/<version>/ so we can roll back.
- Push to main, not only to a separate branch, or neither link updates. GitHub Pages updates about a minute or two after each push.
- No em-dashes in anything Luke reads.
